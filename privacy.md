# Privacy Policy for Liebe
**Last Updated:** November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Liebe** ("the Bot"), created by **Not Gamer**, handles your data on Discord.

## 1. Data We Collect
To provide AI conversations, mini-games, and moderation features, the Bot may temporarily process or store:
* **Discord IDs:** User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs (for game stats, moderation logs, and command cooldowns).
* **Message Content:** Messages sent directly to or mentioning the Bot for AI conversation responses.

## 2. How We Use Data
Collected data is used strictly to:
* Generate AI replies during active conversations.
* Track game scores and leaderboards.
* Execute moderation actions requested by server administrators.

## 3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services
* AI prompts may be processed through third-party AI providers solely to generate responses.
* We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.
* Message content used for AI chat is not permanently stored after a response is generated.

## 4. Data Deletion
You may request the deletion of your stored game stats or server data at any time by contacting **Not Gamer** (`notgamer__69`) on Discord.
